How it works

Three things, no more

It plays where you find it

A YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify or SoundCloud link — or a bare audio, video or image file — turns into a player in the feed. No tab-hopping to hear a track.

The feed is ranked, not sorted

Scrolls weighs what people watched, liked and came back to, decays it with age, and samples the order — so it isn't the same list twice.

Reach pays

Publishing earns XP. Points — the ones that buy cosmetics — come only from other people finding your work: viewers, likes, comments, followers.
